About this site

A place to think in public.

I’m Aleks, an economics student at the University of St. Gallen. I write about the systems I use to study, build projects, and make AI useful in everyday work.

Þaut is an Icelandic word for a rush or a whistle like the wind. It fits what I want these essays to do: carry useful information quickly and leave a clear signal behind.

This site is deliberately small. Each post starts as something I wanted to explain to a friend, then gets written down well enough to share.

Made in Switzerland, one note at a time.
